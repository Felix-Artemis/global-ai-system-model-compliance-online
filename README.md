# global-ai-system-model-compliance-online
A business-facing global compliance assessment Skill for AI systems and models.

The Skill collects factual information about a specific AI platform through either a streamlined or full questionnaire and conducts a structured, multi-layered compliance assessment.

The first layer assesses AI transparency, user notice, and labeling requirements in Mainland China, the European Union, and California, United States.

The second layer evaluates system-level requirements under the EU AI Act, including prohibited AI practices, high-risk classification, and model-level obligations applicable to general-purpose AI (GPAI).

The third layer, where relevant, screens for additional compliance issues, including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, cross-border data transfers, U.S. sensitive-data restrictions, copyright, and product-related regulatory requirements.

The Skill is fact-driven and does not rely on product names, model names, or vendor labels as substitutes for factual investigation. During the assessment, it may retrieve current laws, regulations, and official guidance from relevant jurisdictional sources and use them to generate jurisdiction-specific conditional assessments and follow-up actions.

Outputs include both plain-language risk recommendations and auditable machine-readable results. Conclusions are clearly classified as “Launch Deferred,” “Conditional Launch,” “Unable to Confirm,” or “Launch Permitted (Ongoing Obligations).”

This Skill does not constitute legal advice and does not replace a formal legal review of any specific matter. Its purpose is to help business teams identify compliance risks early, clarify responsibility allocation, and establish a basis for subsequent compliance work.

For a streamlined assessment, please use the `global-ai-system-model-compliance-online-lite` Skill.
