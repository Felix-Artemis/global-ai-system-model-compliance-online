# Online coverage matrix

The URL manifest covers the AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, COM(2025) 836
and SWD(2025) 836, Machinery and UAS materials, all 20 current Annex I
expressions, selected official guidance and Codes of Practice, GDPR,
copyright, ePrivacy, Data Act, Cyber Resilience Act, product liability,
market surveillance and standardisation entry points.

Run `python3 scripts/fetch_official_sources.py --profile all` to retrieve the
full configured set. A successful HTTP response is not by itself proof that a
document is final or legally binding; use the returned URL, content type,
CELEX/ELI/OJ information and current EUR-Lex status when citing it.
